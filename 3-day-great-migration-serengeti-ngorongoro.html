Day 1: Serengeti Ndutu for Migration

Transfer: Arusha – Serengeti Ndutu: 5 hours

Program of the Day: Pick up from your lodge or airport and departure for the Serengeti Ndutu Park for wildlife sighting with special eye to the Great Migration. Enjoy lunch in a suitable place inside the park. In the afternoon we will continue for the game drive activity before reaching the camp.

The Great Migration is the largest herd movement of wildebeests, Zebra and other animals gather together for calving season. Wildebeests give birth at this time. The best time to visit is from mid-December to early April every year.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Sero Ndutu Camp.

Day 2: Serengeti Ndutu & Ngorongoro Conservation

Transfer: Serengeti – Ngorongoro: 2 hours

Program of the Day: Today wake up early and enjoy again the Great Migration game drives in Serengeti Ndutu Park, then in the afternoon departure to Ngorongoro for overnight at the accommodation located on the Ngorongoro Crater Rim. This gives you a chance to descend inside the Crater next day in early morning and see many animals when they are still more active.

Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Sero Mara Tented Camp (Ndutu) / Embalakai Ngorongoro Camp.

Day 3: Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Ngorongoro – Arusha: 3 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, then we descend inside Ngorongoro Crater for game drives. Once we finish game drives, we will transfer you to Arusha or Kilimanjaro airport for your flight back home or next destination.

This Park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards). Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: End of Safari (No Accommodation).
