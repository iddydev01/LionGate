Day 1: Arrival and Transfer to Arusha

Transfer: Kilimanjaro Airport – Arusha town: 1 hour

Program of the Day: Our Safari guide from our office will pick you up at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport and transfer you to your hotel in Arusha for relaxation and overnight. Feel free to experience Arusha city tour with our guide for free.

Activities of the Day: Transfer and Relaxation / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge (Half Board).

Day 2: Arusha - Tarangire National Park

Transfer: Transfer Arusha – Tarangire: 2 hours

Program of the Day: Pick up from the Lodge early morning and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National while carrying lunch boxes.

Tarangire Park is well known for its large elephant herds, consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna with the Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing Lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above. Once game drives is done, we drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 3: Central Serengeti Park

Transfer: Transfer Karatu – Central Serengeti: 3 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, departure for the Central Serengeti Park for wildlife sighting. Enjoy lunch in a suitable place inside the park. In the afternoon we will continue for the game drive activity before reaching the camp. Next day we will spend full day inside this park for game drives.

Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Kontik Serengeti Camp.

Day 4: Northern Serengeti for Migration

Transfer: Central Serengeti – Northern Serengeti: 3 hours

Program of the Day: Get up early and enjoy breakfast, then we drive to Northern Serengeti around Mara River where Great Migration is happening. Our safari guide will find the best spot point for Great Migration.

The Great Migration is the largest herd movement of wildebeests, Zebra and other animals crossing Mara River to Masai Mara in Kenya. Migration crosses Mara River that is full of Crocodiles. The best time to visit is from July to early October every year.

Activities of the Day: Game drives / Accommodation: Sero Mara Tented Camp.

Day 5: Northern Serengeti for Migration

Transfer: Northern Serengeti - Central Serengeti: 3 hours

Program of the Day: Get up early and enjoy breakfast, then continue with game drive in Northern Serengeti with special eye to the Great Migration. Later we drive back to Central Serengeti where we stop for overnight.

The Great Migration is the largest herd movement of wildebeests, Zebra and other animals crossing Mara River to Masai Mara in Kenya. Migration crosses Mara River that is full of Crocodiles. The best time to visit is from July to early October every year.

Activities of the Day: Game drives / Accommodation: Kontik Serengeti Camp.

Day 6: Central Serengeti - Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Transfer Central Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater: 2 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, then depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

This park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards). Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 7: Karatu - Lake Manyara National Park

Transfer: Transfer Karatu – Lake Manyara: 35 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to Arusha for overnight.

The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colony of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park.

Activities of the Day: Game drives / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge (Half Board).

Day 8: Arusha – Arusha/Kilimanjaro Airport

Transfer: Arusha – Kilimanjaro Airport: 1 hour

Program of the Day: After breakfast relax at the lodge and if time allows take a short tour in Arusha for shopping, then transfer to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport.
