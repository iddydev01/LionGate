  • Itinerary: 100% Tailor Made Itinerary.
  • Destinations: Arusha, Tarangire National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, Lake Manyara National Park.
  • Safari Type: Private Safari.
  • Transportation Type: Pop-up roof 4*4 Vehicle Land Cruiser.
  • Tour Guide Driver: Experienced, Knowledgeable, Professional English-Speaking Guide.

OVERVIEW

A 3-days Safari gives you a chance to witness Big Five animals and other interesting Animals. With LIONGATE ADVENTURES you will be immersed in the unspoiled nature of real Africa, you will see wild animals up close and take photos of the wonderful landscapes of Tanzania.

All this with the assistance of a Tour Operator based in Arusha, full board on safari, personal English-speaking guide, and private use of the jeep (with mineral water, power sockets, binoculars) and selected accommodations.

Day 1: Arusha - Tarangire National Park

Transfer: Arusha – Tarangire: 2 hours

Program of the Day: Pick up from the Lodge early morning or airport and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National Park while carrying lunch boxes.

Tarangire Park is well known for its large elephant herds, consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna with the Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing Lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above. Once game drives is done, we drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 2: Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Karatu – Ngorongoro Crater: 40 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

This Park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards). Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 3: Karatu - Lake Manyara National Park

Transfer: Karatu – Lake Manyara: 35 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport where safari ends.

The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colony of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: End of Safari (No Accommodation).
