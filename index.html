WHO WE ARE

YOUR TRUSTED LOCAL SAFARI PARTNER

Your Gateway to Authentic Tanzania Safaris

Safari is more than just watching wildlife from a safari vehicle. The word safari comes from Swahili, meaning “journey,” and in Tanzania every journey tells a story. From the endless plains of the Serengeti to the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, every destination offers a unique opportunity to experience the true beauty of Africa.

At LionGate Adventures, we are passionate about creating unforgettable travel experiences across Tanzania. As a locally owned tour company based in Arusha, we are dedicated to providing safe, authentic, and well-organized safari adventures that allow travelers to explore the wonders of Tanzania in comfort and style.

LionGate Adventures offers a wide range of Tanzania safari experiences including Wildlife Safaris, Cultural Tours, Mountain Trekking, and Beach Holidays in Zanzibar.

Safari packages are carefully designed to suit different travel styles and budgets, ranging from Budget Safaris to Mid-Range and Luxury Safari Packages.

Our safari destinations include some of Tanzania’s most iconic national parks such as Serengeti National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire National Park, Lake Manyara National Park, and other spectacular wildlife areas. These destinations offer incredible opportunities to witness the famous Big Five and the world-renowned Great Wildebeest Migration.

For adventure seekers, we also organize Mount Kilimanjaro trekking through popular routes including the Machame Route, Marangu Route, Lemosho Route, Rongai Route, and other scenic trails.

At LionGate Adventures, our goal is to make your African safari experience smooth, memorable, and truly inspiring. With experienced local guides, personalized itineraries, and a strong commitment to quality service, we ensure every journey with us becomes a lifetime memory.

FAQ SECTION

Frequently asked questions and detailed answers for quick informational assistance.

What is the best time to visit Tanzania?

The best time to visit Tanzania is during the dry season from late June to October, which offers the best wildlife viewing conditions across major national parks.

Do I need a visa to visit Tanzania?

Yes, most foreign nationals require a visa to enter Tanzania. You can apply online for an e-Visa in advance or obtain a visa on arrival at major entry ports.

Is Tanzania safe for tourists?

Yes, Tanzania is generally very safe for tourists. Guided safaris are highly secure, and our experienced tour operators and guides prioritize your safety at all times.

How much does a safari cost?

Safari costs vary depending on the duration, group size, and tier of accommodation (budget camping vs. luxury lodges). Contact us directly for a customized quote tailored to your budget.

Can itineraries be customized?

Absolutely! All of our safari itineraries are 100% customizable. Our experts will work directly with you to craft a trip tailored exactly to your timeline and preferences.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank wire transfers and major credit cards through our secure online payment systems.
