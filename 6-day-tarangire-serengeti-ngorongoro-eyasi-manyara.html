Day 1: Arusha - Tarangire National Park

Transfer: Arusha – Tarangire: 2 hours

Program of the Day: Pick up from the Lodge or airport and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National while carrying lunch boxes.

Tarangire Park is well known for its large elephant herds, consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna with the Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing Lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above. Once game drives is done, we drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 2 & 3: Serengeti National Park

Transfer: Karatu – Serengeti: 3 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, departure for the Serengeti Park for wildlife sighting. Enjoy lunch in a suitable place inside the park. In the afternoon we will continue for the game drive activity before reaching the camp. Next day we will spend full day inside this park for game drives.

Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Kontik Serengeti Camp.

Day 4: Serengeti - Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater: 2 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, then depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

This park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards). Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 5: Karatu – Eyasi for Cultural Experience

Program of the Day: Once you enjoy your breakfast, our safari guide will drive you to Lake Eyasi where you meet the Hadzabe tribe. This tribe is famous for hunting, so you will be able to go hunting with them and see how they do hunting.

Once you are done with Hadzabe, you will be taken to another tribe called the Datoga tribe. This tribe is well-known and experienced in Blacksmiths skills. Furthermore, you will be able to stretch your legs by walking around Lake Eyasi and visit the local village there. After all these activities, you will be taken back to the lodge for relaxation, dinner and your overnight stay.

Activities of the Day: Cultural Experience / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 6: Karatu - Lake Manyara National Park

Transfer: Karatu – Lake Manyara: 35 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport where our service ends.

The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colony of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park.

Activities of the Day: Game drives / Accommodation: End of Safari.
