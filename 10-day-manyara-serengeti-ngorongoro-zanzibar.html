OVERVIEW

A 6-days Safari gives you a chance to witness Big Five animals and other interesting Animals. With LIONGATE ADVENTURES you will be immersed in the unspoiled nature of real Africa, you will see wild animals up close and take photos of the wonderful landscapes of Tanzania.

All this with the assistance of a Tour Operator based in Arusha, full board on safari, personal English-speaking guide, private use of the jeep (with power sockets, fridge with cold drinks and snacks, binoculars) and selected accommodations. After safari you fly to Zanzibar for 4 nights relaxing on the beach.

Day 1: Arrival and Transfer to Arusha

Transfer: Kilimanjaro Airport – Arusha town: 1 hour

Program of the Day: Our Safari guide from our office will pick you up at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport and transfer you to your hotel in Arusha for relaxation and overnight. Feel free to explore the city with our guide for free.

Activities of the Day: Transfer and Relaxation / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge (Half Board).

Day 2: Arusha - Manyara National Park

Transfer: Arusha – Manyara: 2 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to the lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colony of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park.

Activities of the Day: Game drives / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 3 & Day 4: Serengeti National Park

Transfer: Karatu – Serengeti: 3 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, departure for the Serengeti Park for wildlife sighting. Enjoy lunch in a suitable place inside the park. In the afternoon we will continue for the game drive activity before reaching the camp. Next day we will spend full day inside this park for game drives.

Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

Activities of the Day: Game drives & Masai Village Visit. / Accommodation: Kontik Serengeti Camp.

Day 5: Serengeti - Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater: 2 hours

Program of the Day: After breakfast, then depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

This park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards. Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 6: Karatu – Arusha Airport – Stone Town

Transfer: Karatu – Arusha Airport: 2 hours transfer

Program of the Day: After breakfast relax at the lodge and if time allows take a short tour in Karatu town for shopping, then transfer to Arusha Airport and then fly to Zanzibar. Our driver in Zanzibar will be waiting for you at Zanzibar Airport for the private transfer to your hotel in Stone town.

Activities of the Day: Airport transfers / Accommodation: Tembo House Hotel (Standard room, Half Board).

Day 7: Stone Town Tour & Transfer to the beach Hotel

Program of the Day: After breakfast we dedicate this day for Zanzibar Stone tour with our local guide.

Admire the narrow streets lined with many residential houses that preserve their original Swahili-Omani themes with exquisitely carved doorways with metal studs, overhanging balconies and latticed windows. Side by side, you will see churches that are evidently continental due to the colonial presence and the many mosques commissioned by the olden Sultans of Zanzibar. You will also visit other interesting destinations here. Once we are done you will be transferred to your beach hotel.

Activities of the Day: Stone town tour / Accommodation: Smile Beach Hotel (Standard room, Half Board).

Day 8 & 9: Relaxation on the beach

Program of the Day: These days are dedicated to relax on the beach.

Discover Zanzibar's best beaches with its spectacular sunsets, palm-fringed beaches, turquoise waters & colourful coral reefs.

Activities of the Day: Relaxation / Accommodation: Smile Beach Hotel (Standard room, Half Board).

Day 10: Transfer back to Zanzibar Airport

Program of the Day: Pick up on time from your resort and then you will be transferred back to Zanzibar Airport where our service ends.
