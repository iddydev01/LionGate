4 DAY WILDLIFE AND CULTURAL EXPERIENCE SAFARI

4-day Safari gives you a chance to witness Big Five animals and other interesting Animals. You will also be able to meet Hadzabe and Datoga tribe in Lake Eyasi for a cultural experience. All this with the assistance of a Tour Operator based in Arusha, full board on safari, personal English-speaking guide, and private use of the jeep (with mineral water, power sockets, binoculars) and selected accommodations.

DAY-1: Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Program: Pick up from the Lodge early morning and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National for wildlife game drive while carrying lunch boxes. Once game drive is over, we drive back to the lodge for relaxation and overnight.

The name of the park is originated from Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands which is the only source of water for wildlife here during the dry season. The Park is well known for its large elephant herds, consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing Lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge

DAY-2: Lake Eyasi for a Cultural Tour Experience

Program: After enjoy your breakfast, our safari guide will drive you to Lake Eyasi where you meet the Hadzabe tribe, this tribe is famous for hunting, so you will be able to go hunting with them and see how they do hunt. Once you are done with Hadzabe you will be taken to another tribe called Datoga tribe. This tribe is well known and experienced in Blacksmith’s skills.

Furthermore, you will be able to stretch your legs by walking around Lake Eyasi and then visit of local village around there to experience the real Africa life style. After all these activities, you will be taken back to the lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight stays.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

DAY-3: Ngorongoro Crater

Program: After breakfast, our tour guide driver will pick up you and then depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

Ngorongoro Crater is among of UNESCO world heritage site in Africa and is one of the Natural wonders of Africa which constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world, with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos and many varieties of birds such as flamingos, eagles, and buzzards. Be ready to see many animals here within a little time.

Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

DAY-4: Lake Manyara National Park to Arusha/Kilimanjaro Airport

Program: Our tour guide driver we will pick you up from your Lodge after breakfast, and then depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport for your Air transfer back home or to another destination. Where safari ends.

Lake Manyara National Park covers an area of only 330 sq. km (127 sq. miles), of which up to 200 sq. km (77 sq. miles) is made up of the lake when water levels are high. The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colonies of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park. Also the Park is an excellent place for bird watching and a good area to find elephants, Hippo, giraffes, zebras, baboons, warthogs, and buffalos.

Accommodation: No accommodation.
