Program: Day 1

Program of the Day: After breakfast, our safari guide will pick you up from the lodge and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National Park for wildlife game drive while carrying lunch boxes. Once game drives are done, we will drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and an overnight stay.

Tarangire National Park is well known for its large elephant herds, giant scattered Baobab trees and tree-climbing Africa Python. It consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna with the Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands which is the only source of water here for wildlife during the dry season. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing a lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above.

Program: Day 2

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart for the Serengeti National Park for wildlife sightings while carrying lunch boxes. Enjoy lunch in a suitable place inside the park. In the afternoon, we will continue with the game drive activity before reaching the camp. The next day will be spent on full-day game drives inside this park.

Serengeti National Park is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopards, cheetahs, and lions, fulfilling their role in the circle of life. And other wild animals. Once game drives are done, we will drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and an overnight stay.

Activity of the Day: Wildlife Game Drive / Accommodation: Kon Tiki Serengeti Camp.

Program: Day 3

Program of the Day: After breakfast, our tour guide driver will pick up you with picnic lunch boxes and depart to Ngorongoro for a game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater.

Ngorongoro Crater is among of UNESCO world heritage site in Africa and is one of the Natural wonders of Africa which constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world, with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos and many varieties of birds such as flamingos, eagles, and buzzards. Be ready to see many animals here within a little time. Once game drives are done, we will drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and an overnight stay.

Program: Day 4

Program of the Day: After enjoy your breakfast, our safari guide will drive you to Lake Eyasi where you meet the Hadzabe tribe, this tribe is famous for hunting, so you will be able to go hunting with them and see how they do hunt. Once you are done with Hadzabe you will be taken to another tribe called Datoga tribe. This tribe is well known and experienced in Blacksmith’s skills.

Furthermore, you will be able to stretch your legs by walking around Lake Eyasi and then visit local village around there to experience the real Africa life style. After all these activities, you will be taken back to the lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight stays.

Program: Day 5

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive, we will transfer you to Arusha where our service ends.

Lake Manyara National Park covers an area of only 330 sq. km (127 sq. miles), of which up to 200 sq. km (77 sq. miles) is made up of the lake when water levels are high. The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colonies of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park. Also the Park is an excellent place for bird watching and a good area to find elephants, Hippo, giraffes, zebras, baboons, warthogs, and buffalos. At some point during your game drive, you should get off your vehicle at the hippo pool to stretch your legs and observe the hippos here.
