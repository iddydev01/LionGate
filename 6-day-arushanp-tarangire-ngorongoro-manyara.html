Day 1: Arrival and Transfer to Arusha

Transfer: Kilimanjaro Airport – Arusha town: 1 hour

Program of the Day: Our Safari guide from our office will pick you up at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport and transfer you to your hotel in Arusha for relaxation and overnight. We are ready to take you for Arusha city tour to explore this beautiful city.

Activities of the Day: Transfer and Relaxation / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge (Half Board).

Day 2: Arusha town - Arusha National Park

Transfer: Arusha town – Arusha Park: 40 minutes

Program of the Day: Pick up from the Lodge early morning and start a beautiful journey to Arusha National Park while carrying lunch boxes. After game drive you will also explore this park by walking accompanied by park ranger.

The Park is dotted with an abundance of unique mammal species such as the African elephant, Cape buffalo, hippo, zebra, giraffe, and even the camera-shy wildebeest. It has also fascinating landscapes include an expansive open savanna that later gives way to a patch of acacia scrubland before finally culminating in a lush rainforest.

Activities of the Day: Game drives and Walking Safari. / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge.

Day 3: Arusha - Tarangire National Park

Transfer: Arusha – Tarangire: 2 hours

Program of the Day: Pick up from the Lodge early morning and start a beautiful journey to Tarangire National Park while carrying lunch boxes.

Tarangire Park is well known for its large elephant herds, consists of a diversity of landscapes, from seasonal swamps to savanna with the Tarangire River wending its way through the parklands. It is possible that we will have the experience of seeing Lion stalking his prey or leopards watching whilst relaxing in the trees above. Once game drives is done, we drive to your lodge for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 4: Ngorongoro Crater

Transfer: Karatu – Ngorongoro Crater: 40 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, then depart to Ngorongoro for game drive inside Ngorongoro Crater while carrying lunch boxes.

This park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the natural wonders of Africa: with the presence of about 30,000 large mammals resident all year round (such as buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs and rhinos) and many varieties of birds (such as flamingos, eagles, buzzards). Be ready to see many animals here within few time.

Activities of the Day: Game drives. / Accommodation: Marera Valley Lodge.

Day 5: Karatu - Lake Manyara National Park

Transfer: Karatu – Lake Manyara: 35 minutes

Program of the Day: After breakfast, depart to Lake Manyara for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive we will transfer you to Arusha for relaxation, dinner and overnight.

The park is famous for the arboreal lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and the numerous colony of baboons that populate the forest and which will accompany you along the road from the moment you enter the park.

Activities of the Day: Transfer and Relaxation / Accommodation: Arusha Planet Lodge (Half Board).

Day 6: Arusha – Arusha/Kilimanjaro Airport

Transfer: Arusha – Kilimanjaro Airport: 1 hour

Program of the Day: After breakfast relax at the lodge and if time allows take a short tour in Arusha for shopping, then transfer to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport.

Activities of the Day: Departure transfer.
