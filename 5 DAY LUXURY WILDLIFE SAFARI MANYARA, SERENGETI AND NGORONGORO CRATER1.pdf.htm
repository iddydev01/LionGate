5 Day Luxury Wildlife Safari

5 DAY LUXURY WILDLIFE SAFARI MANYARA, SERENGETI AND NGORONGORO CRATER

Overview

This 5-day safari gives you a chance to witness Big Five animals and other interesting animals. You will be immersed in the unspoiled nature of real Africa and sleep in Luxury Accommodations. All this with the assistance of a Tour Operator based in Arusha. Enjoy full board on safari, a Professional personal English-speaking guide, and private use of the jeep with mineral water, power sockets, binoculars, & selected accommodations.

Day 1: Arusha

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by one of our experienced driver guides. You will be conveyed to your accommodation in Arusha. Our partner Arusha Planet lodge is one of the best comfortable and luxury lodges for relaxation. If you have time, you will have a short tour of Arusha city to explore this beautiful tourism city with our guide without any charges.

Accommodation: Mount Meru Hotel in Half board Meal.

Day 2: Lake Manyara National Park

After breakfast, we transfer to Lake Manyara National Park for a game drive while carrying a picnic lunch box. Once finished, we convey you to your accommodation. Lake Manyara Serena Safari Lodge is one of the best comfortable and luxury Lodges to relax.

Lake Manyara National Park is famous for its legendary tree climbing lions, which hide among the branches of the trees, and numerous colonies of baboons. Also, here you will be able to explore huge number of different bird species like flamingos in soda lake and hippos.

Accommodation: Lake Manyara Serena Safari Lodge.

Day 3: Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, you depart for Serengeti National Park for an amazing and beautiful game drive and enjoy lunch at a suitable place inside the park. We will continue the game drive activity in the afternoon before reaching the lodge.

Serengeti National Park is well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators such as leopards, cheetahs, and lions fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

Accommodation: Serengeti Serena Safari Lodge.

Day 4: Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Wake up early and enjoy a fantastic game drive in Serengeti National Park before you depart for Ngorongoro Conservation Area in the afternoon. Your accommodation for the night is on the rim of the Ngorongoro Crater.

This park is among the UNESCO world heritage sites and constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world. About 30,000 large mammals reside here year-round, including buffaloes, lions, elephants, cheetahs, and rhinos. The following morning early, you will descend into the crater.

Accommodation: Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge.

Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater & Departure

After breakfast, you will descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for a game drive. Once you finish, you are transported to Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport for your flight back home or to another destination.
